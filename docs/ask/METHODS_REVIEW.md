## The access measures a ranking can use

- **short** (default): the share of residents 55+ with fewer than 20 recruiting trials within 60 road-miles ("short of trials"). Worst = highest.
- **none**: the share of residents 55+ with no recruiting trial within 60 road-miles. Worst = highest.
- **far_nci**: the share of residents 55+ more than 60 road-miles from an NCI-designated cancer center. Worst = highest.
- **median_nci**: the median road-miles from residents 55+ to the nearest NCI-designated cancer center (farther is worse). Worst = highest.
- **far_broad**: the share of residents 55+ more than 60 road-miles from a broad trial menu (a county with 100 or more recruiting trials). Worst = highest.
- **trials_near**: the average number of recruiting trials within 60 road-miles of residents 55+ (fewer is worse). Worst = lowest.

## Parameters, as the box passes them

- `state`: USPS code or state name ("TX", "Texas").
- `place`: A place id from find_places: county FIPS "48215", district "TX-34", state "TX" or "US".
- `county`: A county FIPS id from find_places ("48215").
- `district`: A district id from find_places ("TX-34").
- `query`: A ZIP code, town or county name as the visitor wrote it.
- `measure`: The access measure to rank by (default short). One of: short, none, far_nci, median_nci, far_broad, trials_near.
- `n`: How many places to list (default 10).
- `cancer_type`: A trial cancer-type key. One of: bladder_urothelial, brain_cns, breast, colorectal, gastric_esophageal, gynecologic, head_neck, kidney, leukemia, liver_biliary, lung, lymphoma, mds_mpn, melanoma_skin, myeloma, neuroendocrine_endocrine, other_named, pancreatic, prostate, sarcoma, multi, other_unclassified.
- `phase`: A trial phase. One of: 1, 1-2, 2, 2-3, 3, 4, NA.
- `site`: An incidence / death-rate site key (default all). One of: all, breast, colorectal, lung, prostate, melanoma_skin, lymphoma, leukemia, pancreatic, liver_biliary, bladder_urothelial, kidney, brain_cns, gynecologic_ovary, gynecologic_uterus, gynecologic_cervix, head_neck, thyroid, esophagus, stomach.
