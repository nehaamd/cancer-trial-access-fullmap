What to do with this list

  1. Talk with your oncologist or care team. They know your diagnosis, prior treatment and test results, which decide eligibility. Bring the NCT numbers.
  2. Ask a trial navigator to check eligibility for you (free). NCI Cancer Information Service: 1-800-4-CANCER (1-800-422-6237) or the NCI clinical trials search, which matches on diagnosis and treatment history. The American Cancer Society's ACS ACTS service (1-800-227-2345, any hour) matches you to trials and connects you with a cancer information specialist. For blood cancers, the Clinical Trial Support Center at Blood Cancer United (formerly The Leukemia & Lymphoma Society; 1-800-955-4572) pairs you with a nurse navigator.
  3. Contact the study site. Each ClinicalTrials.gov record lists the site's contact; the study team screens for eligibility and explains the trial. Ask about travel, lodging and telehealth support — many trials and foundations help with costs.

About these estimates: This tool uses public data to explore geographic availability of cancer clinical trials. Trial listings may be incomplete or outdated, and distances use approximate locations. A listed trial does not establish patient eligibility, current enrollment availability, or access to treatment. See Methods & data for sources, assumptions, and validation status.
