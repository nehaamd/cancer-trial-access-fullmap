Cancer Trial Access Map

Recruiting cancer treatment trials open to patients 55 and older, by county, congressional district and state, measured in road miles from where people live. Built by Neha Agrawal, MD. Key findings · Code and data on GitHub.

Cite as

Agrawal N. Cancer Trial Access Map. https://nehaamd.github.io/cancer-trial-access-fullmap/ (data as of ).

Every view has a link (“Copy link to this view”) that reopens the same place, filters and tab.

Key terms
Eligible trial.
A recruiting, interventional cancer treatment trial on ClinicalTrials.gov with a US site, open to at least one patient aged 55 or older.
Road-miles.
The shortest driving route on the highway network from where people live (each census tract's population center).
Broad menu.
A county with 100 or more eligible trials, the cut-off ASCO's 2024 county analysis used for a “broad portfolio”.
Limited menu.
A county with 20 to 99 eligible trials. This line is the map's own; ASCO's “limited portfolio” was 1 to 99.
NCI-designated center.
A cancer center designated by the National Cancer Institute that treats adult patients.
