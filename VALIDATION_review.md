## Self-checks that are not independent validation

* **Cancer-type classification.** The builder re-read 50 of the 200 sampled trials. First pass 43/50 agreed; the 7 disagreements were rule gaps (CMML and atypical CML counted as leukemia, plasma-cell leukemia as leukemia, gliosarcoma as sarcoma, "unknown primary of the neck" as other, "colorectal + gastrointestinal neoplasms" as colorectal only). Rules were fixed and all 50 then agreed. This shows the rules were applied as intended, not that the rules are right. `out_adult55/qc_cancer_type_sample_200.csv` (200 trials, classifier output shown) is left for a blind hand check by someone who did not build the classifier.
* **Router validation distances** are approximate published Google Maps / AAA values from general reference, as in the Texas build; they were not re-fetched from a routing service.
